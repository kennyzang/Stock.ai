# Episode 24 — 井底之蛙 jǐng dǐ zhī wā (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** someone with a very narrow view of the world.
- **Literal:** a frog at the bottom of a well.
- **Source:** 《庄子·秋水》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

## Characters and props (repeat these exact specs in every image request)
| Who | Spec |
|---|---|
| THE FROG | a small, round, bright-green frog with big golden eyes, a cheeky proud grin and a puffed-out chest; expressive like a comic character. |
| THE SEA TURTLE | a huge, wise old sea turtle with a moss-green shell, kind droopy eyes, a few barnacles and a calm smile; much bigger than the well. |
| THE WELL | an old round stone well in a quiet village courtyard, mossy stones inside, a little water at the bottom; from inside, only a small circle of sky is visible far above. |
| SETTING | ancient China, soft daylight; the sea (in I7) is an endless calm ocean with soft grey ink-wash waves. |
