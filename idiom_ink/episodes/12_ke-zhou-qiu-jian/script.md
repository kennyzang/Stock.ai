# Episode 12 — 刻舟求剑 kè zhōu qiú jiàn (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** sticking to old methods after things have changed.
- **Literal:** carve the boat to find the sword.
- **Source:** 《吕氏春秋·察今》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~25 s (cap 29.5 s).

| THE FERRYMAN | short old man about 60, wrinkled tanned face, white wispy goatee, conical straw hat, slate-blue short jacket, trousers rolled up, holds a long bamboo pole. |
| THE BOAT | one small flat wooden ferry boat, warm brown planks, low sides. The notch: one simple V-shaped cut carved into the top edge of the boat's side (no letters, no symbols). |
| SETTING | a wide, calm river with soft grey ink-wash mountains in the distance; for the shore scenes, a simple wooden jetty and reeds. |
