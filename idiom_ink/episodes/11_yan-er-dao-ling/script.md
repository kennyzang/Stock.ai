# Episode 11 — 掩耳盗铃 yǎn ěr dào líng (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** fooling only yourself; pretending a problem isn't there.
- **Literal:** cover your ears to steal a bell.
- **Source:** 《吕氏春秋·自知》 (public domain; the original has a bell, 钟), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~25 s (cap 29.5 s).

| THE WATCHMAN (only in I1, I7 and, in modern clothes, I9) | tall broad man about 45, square jaw, neat grey beard, moss-green robe, round black cap, carries a paper lantern (plain, no writing) on a pole. |
| THE BELL | one huge bronze temple bell, taller than Gou's waist, hanging from a dark wooden frame, green-brown patina, plain raised bands (no inscriptions, no characters). The mallet: a big plain wooden mallet. |
| SETTING | night, the overgrown courtyard of a ruined noble house: cracked grey flagstones, weeds, a crumbling pale wall (no writing on it), moonlight; in some images the dark roofs of the town behind the wall. |
