# Episode 23 — 守株待兔 shǒu zhū dài tù (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** waiting for luck instead of working for it.
- **Literal:** guard the tree stump and wait for a rabbit.
- **Source:** 《韩非子·五蠹》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

## Characters and props (repeat these exact specs in every image request)
| Who | Spec |
|---|---|
| THE FARMER | stocky man about 35, round face, short stubbly beard, a straw hat hanging on his back, patched ochre tunic, rolled-up brown trousers, a wooden hoe. |
| THE RABBIT | a plump grey-brown wild rabbit with long ears; after it bumps into the stump it is only dazed, with little cartoon stars circling its head (comic, never hurt-looking, no blood). |
| THE STUMP AND FIELD | one big old tree stump with a few roots at the edge of his small field; a little thatched farmhouse nearby. |
| VILLAGER (only in I1 and I7) | a cheerful old man with a long white beard, grey tunic and a basket on his back. |
