# Episode 06 — 瓜田李下 guā tián lǐ xià

## Script

**Hook (0:00–0:03)** — S01 farmer pointing, scholar crouched among melons. Big text frame 1: **HE DIDN'T STEAL ANYTHING.**
- N: "He didn't steal a thing… so why did the whole village call him a thief?"

**Story**
- S02 road: N: "A young scholar was walking home on a hot summer day."
- S03 melon field edge: N: "His sandal strap broke, right next to a melon field."
- S04 crouching among melons, retying the strap: N: "So he bent down to fix it." Label: **innocent**
- S05 farmer far away, sees only the bent back among melons: N: "But the farmer saw a man crouching in his melons." Label: **looks like stealing**
- S06 farmer running, hoe raised: N (farmer's words, narrator voice): "Hey! Thief!"
- S07 scholar stands, empty hands open, farmer glaring: N: "He showed his empty hands. The farmer didn't believe him."
- S08 plum tree, wind, scholar's cap tilts: N: "A bit later, the wind knocked his hat crooked, right under a plum tree."
- S09 scholar raises both hands to his cap under the branches: N: "He reached up to fix it." Label: **innocent**
- S10 granny with basket, shocked: N: "And an old woman shouted: 'He's picking my plums!'" Label: **looks like stealing**
- S11 scholar walking on alone, thoughtful, sunset: N: "That night he remembered an old poem."
- QUOTE (clean overlay on S11/S08 landscape): 瓜田不纳履，李下不正冠 / guā tián bù nà lǚ, lǐ xià bù zhèng guān. M reads it. N: "In a melon field, don't tie your shoe. Under a plum tree, don't fix your hat."

**Payoff (0:45–0:57)** — landscape (13 s)
- N: "That's the saying…" M: "瓜田李下。"
- Breakdown (M one line `瓜。[pause 0.8s]田。[pause 0.8s]李。[pause 0.8s]下。`, split on silences; NF glosses): 瓜 "melon" · 田 "field" · 李 "plum" · 下 "under".
- Meaning line on screen: **= a spot where innocent acts look guilty**. N: "It means: stay out of spots where innocent things look guilty."

**Use it (0:57–1:10)** — cat mascot on paper panels (8 s each)
1. 你表弟来应聘，你最好回避，瓜田李下。 / nǐ biǎodì lái yìngpìn, nǐ zuìhǎo huíbì, guā tián lǐ xià. / "Your cousin applied for the job? Step off the hiring panel. 瓜田李下."
2. 考试时别看手机，哪怕只看时间，瓜田李下嘛。 / kǎoshì shí bié kàn shǒujī, nǎpà zhǐ kàn shíjiān, guā tián lǐ xià ma. / "Don't check your phone in an exam, even just for the time. 瓜田李下."

**Loop (1:10–1:13)** — cut back to S01.
- N: "Being innocent isn't enough. Sometimes you have to look innocent, too."
