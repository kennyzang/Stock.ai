# Episode 18 — 叶公好龙 yè gōng hào lóng (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** claiming to love something you are actually afraid of.
- **Literal:** Lord Ye loves dragons.
- **Source:** 《新序·杂事》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).
