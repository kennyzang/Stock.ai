# Episode 10 — 画蛇添足 huà shé tiān zú (pilot of the Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** to ruin something by adding what isn't needed; overdoing it.
- **Source:** 《战国策·齐策二》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~25 s (cap 29.5 s). VO ≈ 70 words at ~1.15×.
