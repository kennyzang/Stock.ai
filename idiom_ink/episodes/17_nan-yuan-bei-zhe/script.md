# Episode 17 — 南辕北辙 nán yuán běi zhé (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** doing things that take you further from your goal.
- **Literal:** the cart points south, its tracks go north.
- **Source:** 《战国策·魏策四》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

## Characters and props (repeat these exact specs in every image request)
| Who | Spec |
|---|---|
| THE RICH MAN | plump, very confident man about 45, bushy black beard, tall black official hat, purple silk robe with gold trim, a big green jade ring, a huge grin. |
| JI LIANG, the friend | thin, wise man about 50, short grey beard, calm worried eyes, plain grey-green robe, bamboo walking stick. |
| THE CHARIOT | one two-wheeled wooden chariot pulled by one big, powerful white horse, driven by a young muscular driver (red headband, brown short jacket). The driver is always a small figure. |
| SETTING | a long straight dusty road across flat open country with soft grey ink-wash mountains on the horizon; a plain wooden road post shaped like an arrow (no writing). |
