# Episode 14 — 狐假虎威 hú jiǎ hǔ wēi (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** showing off or bullying with someone else's power.
- **Literal:** the fox borrows the tiger's power.
- **Source:** 《战国策·楚策一》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

## Characters and props (repeat these exact specs in every image request)
| Who | Spec |
|---|---|
| THE FOX | slim red fox, orange-red fur, white chest, black-tipped ears and legs, big bushy tail with a white tip, narrow sly amber eyes, very expressive smug face. A real animal on four legs (no clothes) in the forest scenes. |
| THE TIGER | huge tiger, orange fur with bold black stripes, white face markings, big paws, round expressive eyes (comic, never scary or bloody). A real animal on four legs (no clothes) in the forest scenes. |
| OTHER ANIMALS (only in I1 and I6) | a deer, a rabbit, a wild boar and a monkey, painted in the same style. |
| SETTING | a misty mountain forest in ink-wash style: tall pines, mossy rocks, ferns, a winding dirt forest path. |
