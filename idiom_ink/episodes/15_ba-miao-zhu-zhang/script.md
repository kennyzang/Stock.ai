# Episode 15 — 拔苗助长 bá miáo zhù zhǎng (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** ruining something by rushing it.
- **Literal:** pull up the sprouts to help them grow.
- **Source:** 《孟子·公孙丑上》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).
