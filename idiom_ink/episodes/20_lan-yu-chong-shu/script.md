# Episode 20 — 滥竽充数 làn yú chōng shù (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** faking it among people who really have the skill.
- **Literal:** a fake pipe player fills a seat.
- **Source:** 《韩非子·内储说上》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

## Characters and props (repeat these exact specs in every image request)
| Who | Spec |
|---|---|
| THE FAKER | skinny man about 40, long face, thin pointy goatee, shifty sideways eyes, sly grin, the only musician in a rust-red robe with a black sash and a small black musician's cap. |
| THE YU | a Chinese bamboo mouth organ: a cluster of tall thin bamboo pipes standing in a round wooden base with a short mouthpiece. Every musician holds one. |
| THE KINGS | the OLD KING is grey-bearded and kindly; the NEW KING is young, clean-shaven, with sharp curious eyes. Both wear a golden robe and a flat crown with a beaded fringe. |
| SETTING | a grand palace music hall with red pillars and a polished floor; the other musicians all wear identical slate-blue robes and are simple, smaller background figures. |
