# Episode 21 — 邯郸学步 hán dān xué bù (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** copying others so much that you lose your own skills.
- **Literal:** learning to walk in Handan.
- **Source:** 《庄子·秋水》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

| THE HANDAN LOCALS | an elegant tall man and woman in long flowing pale-ochre and slate-blue silk robes, walking gracefully with perfect posture and calm, refined faces. |

| SETTING | ancient China; his home village is simple and rural, with soft grey ink-wash hills. |
