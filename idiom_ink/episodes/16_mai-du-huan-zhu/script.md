# Episode 16 — 买椟还珠 mǎi dú huán zhū (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** caring about the wrapping and missing what really matters.
- **Literal:** buy the box and give back the pearl.
- **Source:** 《韩非子·外储说左上》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).
