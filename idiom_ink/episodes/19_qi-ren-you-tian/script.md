# Episode 19 — 杞人忧天 qǐ rén yōu tiān (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** worrying about things that will never happen.
- **Literal:** the man of Qi worries about the sky.
- **Source:** 《列子·天瑞》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).

|---|---|

| THE FRIEND | cheerful, stocky man about 40, round smiling face, short black beard, ochre robe, relaxed and calm. |
| THE WORRIER'S HOME | a small village house with a courtyard, a low wooden table, a simple bed under a round window. |
