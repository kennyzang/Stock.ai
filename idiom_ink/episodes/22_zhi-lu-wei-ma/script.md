# Episode 22 — 指鹿为马 zhǐ lù wéi mǎ (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** deliberately twisting the truth and making others go along with it.
- **Literal:** point at a deer and call it a horse.
- **Source:** 《史记·秦始皇本纪》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).
