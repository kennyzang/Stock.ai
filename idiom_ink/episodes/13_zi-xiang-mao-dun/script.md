# Episode 13 — 自相矛盾 zì xiāng máo dùn (Shorts format, see FORMAT_SHORTS.md)

- **Everyday meaning:** contradicting yourself.
- **Literal:** your own spear against your own shield.
- **Source:** 《韩非子·难一》 (public domain), retold in our own words.
- **Target length:** ~30 s (cap 39.5 s).
